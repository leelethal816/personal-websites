01 About

Language is more
than words.
It’s how we connect.

I’m Jacqueline, an educator with an MA in Spanish, Linguistics track, and a BA in Linguistics from Baylor University.

I’m interested in how people learn languages, how classrooms can welcome different experiences, and how language helps us take part in our communities. My experience spans adult education, undergraduate Spanish instruction, academic research, writing, and community-based learning.

Before finding my way to linguistics, I spent three years in professional cello training during my undergraduate studies. I moved toward work where language and intercultural understanding are part of everyday life. That curiosity continues to shape how I teach and learn.
